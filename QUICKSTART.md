# Quickstart

## Today

1. **Browse** `pdfs/textbook/reference/` — open these three first:
   - `14-austrian-german.pdf` — expressions you'll use tomorrow
   - `08-modal-verbs.pdf` — the engine of polite German
   - `10-konjunktiv-ii.pdf` — how to ask things without sounding blunt
