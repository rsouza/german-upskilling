# An der schönen blauen Donau
*Johann Strauss II (1867) | Public domain | Level: B1*

The unofficial anthem of Vienna. Johann Strauss II composed this waltz in 1867; the words were added by poet Josef Weyl. It is played every year at midnight on New Year's Eve in Vienna — after the Pummerin bell of St. Stephen's Cathedral rings, this waltz begins. Every Viennese knows it.

---

## Lyrics & Translation

| German | English |
|--------|---------|
| **Strophe 1** | **Verse 1** |
| Donau so blau, so blau, | Danube so blue, so blue, |
| durch Tal und Au | through valley and meadow |
| wogst ruhig du hin. | you flow peacefully along. |
| Dich grüßen wir, | We greet you, |
| wo immer wir ziehn. | wherever we travel. |
| **Strophe 2** | **Verse 2** |
| Wien, Wien, nur du allein | Vienna, Vienna, you alone |
| sollst stets die Stadt meiner Träume sein! | shall always be the city of my dreams! |
| Dort, wo die schönen Wässer fließen, | There, where the beautiful waters flow, |
| Wien, du Stadt meiner Träume. | Vienna, you city of my dreams. |
| **Strophe 3** | **Verse 3** |
| Wenn auch die Jahre vergeh'n, | Even as the years go by, |
| wird man das nie vergessen, | one will never forget this, |
| was man geliebt, was man geseh'n: | what one has loved, what one has seen: |
| die Donau, die Donau, die wunderbare! | the Danube, the Danube, the wonderful one! |

| German | English | Note |

## Grammar Highlights

**sollst stets die Stadt sein** — *sollen* (modal) + infinitive at end:
- *Du sollst… sein* = You shall be…
- Modal verbs with *du*: *du sollst, du kannst, du musst*

**Wenn auch die Jahre vergeh'n** — *wenn* (even if / as) in a concessive sense:
- *Wenn auch* = even though / even as
- Verb goes to end of clause: *vergeh'n* (= *vergehen*)

**was man geliebt, was man geseh'n** — *man* (one / people) in the past:
- *man hat geliebt* → *man geliebt* (Perfekt without auxiliary in poetry)

## Cultural Note

**Silvester in Wien** — New Year's Eve in Vienna is inseparable from this waltz. At midnight:
1. The *Pummerin* bell of the Stephansdom rings
2. *An der schönen blauen Donau* begins playing on ORF (Austrian national TV)
3. Viennese waltz across the city

If you are still in Vienna next 31. Dezember, stand somewhere with a view of the city at midnight and listen. This is the moment when knowing these words means something.

**The Danube in Vienna** — The Danube (*die Donau*) in central Vienna is actually the *Donaukanal* (a side arm). The main Danube runs along the edge of the city. The Donauinsel (Danube Island) is where Viennese go for cycling, swimming, and the Donauinselfest.
