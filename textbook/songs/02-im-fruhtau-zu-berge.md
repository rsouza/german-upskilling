# Im Frühtau zu Berge
*Traditional German/Austrian folk song | Public domain | Level: A1*

A classic hiking song (*Wanderlied*), perfect for beginners. Simple vocabulary, repetitive structure, strong rhythm. Very well known in Austria — you may hear it at school events or Heuriger evenings.

## Vokabular

| German | English | Note |
|--------|---------|------|
| der Frühtau | morning dew | *früh* (early) + *Tau* (dew) |
| zu Berge ziehen | to head to the mountains | *ziehen* = to move/pull/go |
| grünen | to turn green | *grün* = green |
| der Wald (Wälder) | forest (pl.) | very common noun |
| die Höhe (-n) | height / highland | |
| wandern | to hike / wander | very Austrian! |
| die Sorge (-n) | worry / concern | *ohne Sorgen* = without worries |
| frühzeitig | early | *früh* + *zeitig* |
| wehren | to stop / prevent | *wehrn* = dialect contraction |
| das Glöcklein | little bell | diminutive of *die Glocke* |
| klingen | to ring / sound | *es klingt* = it rings |
| schlagen | to strike (clock) | *die Uhr schlägt* = the clock strikes |

## Grammar Highlights

**im Frühtau** — *in + dem* contracted to *im*; Dative because *in* here = location (when?)
- *im Morgen, im Herbst, im Sommer* — all Dative with *in*

**wir ziehn** — poetic contraction of *wir ziehen* (old-fashioned; you'll see this in songs and poetry)

**des Morgens** — Genitive used for time: *des Morgens* = in the morning (literary/song usage)
- Modern equivalent: *morgens* or *am Morgen*

---

## Austrian Note

*Wandern* (hiking) is central to Austrian culture. The idea of *ohne Sorgen wandern* — carefree hiking — is deeply embedded in Austrian identity. The word *wandern* appears constantly: in conversation (*Wir gehen wandern.*), in idioms, and in company retreat invitations.

**Practical:** *Ich gehe gern wandern.* — I like hiking. (Use this if someone invites you to a *Betriebsausflug*.)
