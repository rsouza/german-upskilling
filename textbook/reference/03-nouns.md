# Nouns — Nomen

---

## Gender (das Genus)

Every German noun has a gender: masculine (**der**), feminine (**die**), or neuter (**das**).  
Learn each noun with its article. *der Tisch, die Tür, das Fenster.*

→ See gender patterns in [Articles & Cases](02-articles-cases.md).

---

## Plurals — Common Patterns

There's no single rule, but these patterns cover most nouns:

| Pattern | Singular | Plural | Notes |
|---------|----------|--------|-------|
| **-e** | der Tag | die Tag**e** | most masc/neut 1-syllable nouns |
| **-er** (+ umlaut often) | das Kind | die Kind**er** | common neuter nouns |
| **-en / -n** | die Frau | die Frau**en** | most feminine nouns |
| **-s** | das Auto | die Auto**s** | foreign words, abbreviations |
| **umlaut only** | der Vater | die V**ä**ter | many masc. with a/o/u |
| **no change** | der Lehrer | die Lehrer | masc. nouns ending in *-er/-el/-en* |
| **-nen** | die Lehrerin | die Lehrerin**nen** | feminine agent nouns |

> In Dative plural, add **-n** to the noun if the plural doesn't already end in *-n* or *-s*:  
> *mit den Kindern / mit den Autos* (not *Autosn*)

---

## Case Endings on Nouns

Nouns themselves only change in a few situations:

**Genitive masculine/neuter:** add **-s** or **-es**
- *des Chef**s**, des Büro**s**, des Mann**es***

**Dative plural:** add **-n** (if plural doesn't end in -n or -s)
- *mit den Kind**ern**, mit den Tag**en***

**Weak masculine nouns (n-Declension):** add **-n/-en** in ALL cases except Nominative
- *der Kollege → des/dem/den Kollegen*
- *der Mensch → des/dem/den Menschen*
- *der Herr → des Herrn, dem Herrn, den Herrn*
- Other examples: *der Kunde, der Journalist, der Präsident, der Student*

---

## Compound Nouns

German loves combining nouns. The last noun determines the gender.

- *der Konferenz**raum*** (die Konferenz + der Raum → masculine)
- *die Arbeits**besprechung*** (die Arbeit + die Besprechung → feminine)
- *das Besprechungs**protokoll*** → neuter

| Masculine | Feminine | English |
|-----------|----------|---------|
| der Kollege | die Kollegin | colleague |
| der Chef | die Chefin | boss |
| der Direktor | die Direktorin | director |
| der Arzt | die Ärztin | doctor |
| der Lehrer | die Lehrerin | teacher |
| der Österreicher | die Österreicherin | Austrian |

> In Austria, using **beide Formen** (Kolleginnen und Kollegen) or the gender-neutral **Kolleg:innen** is common in professional settings.
