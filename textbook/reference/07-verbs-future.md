# Verbs: Future — Zukunft

---

## Two Ways to Express Future

### Option 1: Present Tense + Time Word (most common in speech)

Germans often use the **present tense** for future events when the time is clear from context.

- *Ich **komme** morgen ins Büro.* — I'm coming to the office tomorrow.
- *Wir **haben** nächste Woche ein Meeting.* — We have a meeting next week.
- *Das Meeting **beginnt** um 10 Uhr.* — The meeting starts at 10.

> This is **very natural** in Austrian German. When in doubt, use this.

---

### Option 2: werden + Infinitiv (Zukunft I)

Structure: **werden** (position 2) + infinitive (at the end)

| | werden |
|-|--------|
| ich | **werde** |
| du | **wirst** |
| er/sie/es | **wird** |
| wir | **werden** |
| ihr | **werdet** |
| sie/Sie | **werden** |

**When to use *werden*:**
- Predictions / assumptions about the future
- Promises or formal intentions
- When the future needs to be emphasised
- Assumptions about the present: *Er **wird** noch im Büro **sein**.* (He's probably still in the office.)

**Examples:**
- *Ich **werde** das Dokument morgen **schicken**.* — I will send the document tomorrow.
- *Das **wird** nicht einfach **sein**.* — That won't be easy.
- *Sie **wird** wahrscheinlich zu spät **kommen**.* — She will probably be late.
- *Wir **werden** eine Lösung **finden**.* — We will find a solution.

---

## Zukunft II (Future Perfect — B2)

For something that **will have been completed** by a future point.  
**werden + Partizip II + haben/sein**

- *Bis Freitag **werde** ich den Bericht **fertig geschrieben haben**.* — By Friday I will have finished writing the report.

> This tense is rare in spoken Austrian German — just be able to recognise it.

## Useful Future Expressions

| German | English |
|--------|---------|
| morgen | tomorrow |
| übermorgen | the day after tomorrow |
| nächste Woche | next week |
| nächsten Monat | next month |
| nächstes Jahr | next year |
| bald | soon |
| gleich | in a moment / right away |
| später | later |
| in einer Stunde | in an hour |
| in zwei Wochen | in two weeks |
| ab nächster Woche | from next week onwards |
| demnächst | soon / in the near future |

---

## Meeting & Work Context

- *Das Meeting **wird** ca. eine Stunde **dauern**.* — The meeting will last about an hour.
- *Ich **schicke** Ihnen die Unterlagen morgen.* — I'll send you the documents tomorrow. (present for future)
- *Wir **werden** das nächste Woche **besprechen**.* — We'll discuss that next week.
- *Ich **melde** mich dann bei Ihnen.* — I'll be in touch. (lit. I'll get back to you)
