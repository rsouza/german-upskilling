# Resources — Ressourcen

Curated external resources. Quality over quantity — these are the ones worth using.

---

## Listening (Daily Habit — 15 min)

### Slow German ⭐ (Start Here)
- **URL**: https://slowgerman.com
- **Why**: Austrian accent (Annik Rubens is from Bavaria/Austria), clear speech, free, transcripts available
- **Level**: A2–B2 (pick episodes by topic)
- **How to use**: Listen once at full speed, then again with transcript open

### Nicos Weg (DW)
- **URL**: https://www.dw.com/de/deutsch-lernen/nicos-weg/s-52100
- **Why**: Free, structured A1→B1 video series, excellent for German daily life contexts
- **Level**: A1–B1 (start at the right level)

### Deutsch – Warum nicht? (DW)
- **URL**: https://www.dw.com/de/deutsch-lernen/warum-nicht/s-2548
- **Why**: Classic DW series, good for listening to natural-paced German
- **Level**: A2–B1

## Grammar Reference (Online)

### dict.cc
- **URL**: https://www.dict.cc
- **Why**: Best German–English dictionary, includes Austrian usage notes

### Canoo / Duden
- **URL**: https://www.duden.de
- **Why**: Authoritative German dictionary and grammar, in German

### Verbix
- **URL**: https://www.verbix.com/languages/german.html
- **Why**: Full conjugation tables for any German verb

---

## Practice Apps

### Anki
- **URL**: https://apps.ankiweb.net (free, desktop + mobile)
- **Why**: Spaced repetition for vocabulary — the most evidence-based memorisation method
- **How to use**: Add 5 new cards per session from the master vocabulary list

### Babbel (optional, paid)
- **Why**: More structured than Duolingo, better grammar explanations
- **Note**: Better than Duolingo for adult learners, but our sessions replace this

## Speaking Practice (When Ready — A2 Stage)

### Tandem App
- **URL**: https://www.tandem.net
- **Why**: Language exchange — find Austrian German speakers who want to practise English/Portuguese
- **How**: 30 min German / 30 min English, voice or video call

### iTalki
- **URL**: https://www.italki.com
- **Why**: Book affordable community tutors for free conversation practice
- **Search for**: Austrian German tutor, 1×/week, 30 min conversation

---

## Austrian-Specific

### ORF TVthek
- **URL**: https://tvthek.orf.at
- **Why**: Austrian public TV — real Austrian German, news, documentaries
- **For B1+**: Try watching the news (*ZIB*) with Austrian subtitles

### Wien Geschichte Wiki
- **URL**: https://www.geschichtewiki.wien.gv.at
- **Why**: Read about Vienna in German — interesting content motivates reading practice
